Standard errors are clustered by country and year. All coefficients have been multiplied by 100 for presentation, and standard errors are in parentheses.  In~Columns~(4)-(6) some observations are lost due to there only being one observation in a region-year or in a continent-regime-year and from missing control observations. \sym{***}, \sym{**}, \sym{*} denote significance at the 1\%, 5\%, and 10\% level, respectively.
